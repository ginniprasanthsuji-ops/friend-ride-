FRIEND RIDE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This draft requires business/legal review before production publication.

FRIEND RIDE provides ride, delivery, marketplace, home-service, laundry and related services.

Information collected

  • Account name and mobile number for OTP authentication.
  • Profile, role, booking, ride, order, service, complaint and transaction details.
  • Address and location when a location feature is used, including pickup/drop selection and driver tracking.
  • Verification information and documents for driver, partner and service-provider onboarding.
  • Device/browser diagnostics and notification subscription data for reliability and security.

Use

Information is used to authenticate users, match and provide services, support bookings and tracking, process service/payment records, verify providers, communicate service updates, prevent abuse, troubleshoot and meet legal requirements.

Location

Location is used for features you request or enable. If device location is denied, manual address entry may be available; location-dependent features may not work.

Sharing

Necessary information may be shared with ride/order participants, drivers, partners, service providers, administrators and technical providers (hosting, maps, authentication, messaging, notifications and payments) to complete services, support users, protect the service or comply with law. Only relevant service information should be shared.

Payments

Payment processing is handled by payment providers or methods shown in the app. FRIEND RIDE should not store full card credentials. Transaction references and status records may be retained for reconciliation and support.

Retention and security

Data is retained as needed for service operation, safety, disputes, financial/legal records and applicable requirements. Access controls and private storage/policies are used where configured. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and deletion

You may request correction or deletion of eligible information, subject to lawful retention, safety, fraud and dispute requirements. See Account Deletion Request. Device settings can be used to manage location and notifications.

Children

The service is not intended for children to create accounts independently without appropriate guardian involvement. Contact support if you believe a child's data was submitted improperly.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@friendride.in.
