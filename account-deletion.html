FRIEND RIDE Account Deletion Request

Last updated: 22 November 2026

To request deletion of your FRIEND RIDE account and associated personal information, email support@friendride.in with subject FRIEND RIDE Account Deletion Request.

Include the mobile number registered to your account. Do not email OTPs, passwords, Aadhaar numbers, payment-card details or other sensitive documents. We may verify the request through the registered mobile number.

Process

  1. We verify account ownership.
  2. We review records that may need to be retained for legal, tax, financial, safety, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.
  3. Eligible account and personal data will be deleted or anonymized. Records required by law or necessary for legitimate safety/financial recordkeeping may be retained for the required period and restricted from other use.
  4. We will provide a response about the request and any applicable retention.

Important: An automated in-app deletion feature is not yet available. This page provides an email request route; the business must implement and operationalize its deletion-request workflow before Play Store release.
