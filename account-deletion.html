FRIEND RIDE Account Deletion Request

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

Request account and eligible personal-data deletion by emailing support@friendride.in with subject FRIEND RIDE Account Deletion Request. Include the mobile number associated with the account. Do not email OTPs, passwords, Aadhaar/card details or sensitive documents.

Process

  1. We verify account ownership using the registered number.
  2. We review related safety, dispute, legal and financial records.
  3. Eligible account data is deleted or anonymized. Records that must be retained for law, tax, safety, fraud or dispute purposes may be retained for the required period and restricted.
  4. We reply with the request outcome and any retention explanation.

This is a manual request channel, not automated in-app deletion. The business must establish and follow a response process before release.
