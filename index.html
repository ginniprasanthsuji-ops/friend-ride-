# FRIEND RIDE V8 — Consolidated Feature Plan **Status:** Feature decisions consolidated for the next build and back-test **Platform:** Supabase + HTML/PWA **Important:** This is the agreed V8 scope. It is not a claim that every feature is already implemented or tested. ## 1. App structure and experience - One FRIEND RIDE Super App with separate Customer, Driver, Partner and Admin dashboards. - Retain the premium V7 foundation and the floating **FR Super App** launcher. - Customer screens must not expose SQL, RLS, approval rules, admin controls, API secrets or internal errors. - Admin CRM: customer 360, customer totals/segments, follow-ups, interactions, partner/driver management, orders, revenue, complaints, refunds, settlements, subscriptions, notification logs, GST/platform settings and audit history. - Admin can add/edit categories and subcategories. ## 2. Services and catalogue - Rides: Bike, Auto, Car. - Food, restaurants, tiffin, bakery. - Pharmacy/medical products, with prescription and applicable licence checks. - Grocery/general store, local retail, electronics/electrical, ladies accessories, chicken/fresh products. - Local services: electrician, mason, plumber, AC mechanic, septic tank service. - Parcel delivery. - Partners manage only their own shop, products, images, prices, stock, variants, brand/model and warranty. ## 3. Multi-shop checkout and fulfilment - **One Cart – One Checkout – Multiple Shop Orders.** - One parent checkout creates a separate child order for each shop; each child has its own Order ID, status, invoice, pickup/delivery and settlement. - Customer sees all child orders together on one Orders screen. - Each partner sees only their own child order. - Partner acceptance is atomic: exactly one eligible partner can accept a child order. - Priority-1 partners are notified simultaneously. If no response in 5 minutes, escalate to the next priority group. Rejections also route to the next eligible partner. - A driver delivery request is sent only after the partner marks that child order Ready for Pickup. - Driver acceptance is atomic: only one driver can accept each delivery. - If no driver accepts, retry/escalate using an Admin-configurable policy. ## 4. Delivery charges - 0–3 KM: ₹50 - Above 3 KM to 8 KM: ₹80 - Above 8 KM: ₹120 - Applies to the agreed local delivery categories. Admin should be able to configure rates later. ## 5. Delivery handover OTP - Generate a separate six-digit OTP for each shop child order when delivery reaches the customer / handover stage. - Driver enters the OTP in the Driver Dashboard. - Do not mark Delivered unless the OTP is verified. - Wrong OTP must not complete delivery. Add expiry, attempt limits, rate limiting and verification logs. - Ride booking itself does not require this delivery OTP. ## 6. Notifications - In-App + Background Push. WhatsApp API is not part of the initial build. - Supabase Realtime for active sessions; PWA Service Worker + Web Push/FCM + server-side Edge Function for background push. - Notification inbox/history, tap-through to the relevant order/ride, sound/vibration where supported, retry logs and deduplication. - Push depends on browser/device support, permission, network and battery settings; never promise guaranteed delivery. ## 7. Billing, GST and PDF invoices - Automatically generate a PDF invoice for each shop child order at the correct invoice point. - Each seller issues its own GST Tax Invoice or Bill of Supply, as applicable. Do not combine multiple sellers into one seller invoice. - FRIEND RIDE platform/service fee invoice is separate, where applicable. - Customer Orders page: view, download and share PDF; provide a WhatsApp share action using the device share sheet where supported. A WhatsApp Business API integration is not required initially. - Add QR verification on each invoice. QR contains a random verification token / verification URL, not customer secrets. Public verification shows only minimal invoice authenticity details. - Keep invoice number series, issue date, seller legal name/address/GSTIN, customer details as applicable, line items, HSN/SAC, quantity, taxable value, discount, tax rate, CGST/SGST or IGST, totals and required declarations. - Capture seller GST registration type, GSTIN, HSN/SAC and applicable tax rates in partner settings. Admin review before use. - Keep immutable invoice snapshots. Corrections/refunds use credit notes or other legally appropriate documents; do not silently overwrite an issued invoice. - Invoice history in Customer, Partner and Admin dashboards. - Admin GST reports by seller, period, tax type, refunds and settlements. - GST applicability, E-Invoice, E-Way Bill, TCS and platform service tax treatment must be validated with a CA against the final business model. Do not invent tax rates. ## 8. Platform fee - Proposed agreed formula: `min(₹5, max(₹2, order_value × 2%))`. - Illustrations: ₹50 → ₹2; ₹100 → ₹2; ₹250 → ₹5; ₹500 and above → ₹5. - Delivery fee is separate. - Show fee transparently before payment. - Fee should be charged once per unified checkout (not once per shop child order) if this is the final confirmed business rule. Keep the setting configurable pending final confirmation. - Ledger must distinguish seller payable, driver delivery earning, platform fee, gateway charges, refunds and COD collection. Gross platform fee is not net profit. ## 9. COD, UPI and settlements - COD: driver records cash collected; ledger splits seller payable, driver delivery earning and platform fee according to the approved settlement model. - Daily cash remittance, reconciliation, mismatch alerts and seller settlement reports. - UPI/payment success must be verified server-side with the payment provider. Screenshots or customer-entered UTR alone are not proof of payment. - Keep seller invoice and platform service invoice separate. ## 10. Subscriptions - Driver subscription: ₹500 (current planned price). - Partner subscription: ₹299 (current planned price). - Three-day grace period after expiry. During grace, existing accounts may continue accepting new rides/orders. - After grace ends without renewal, block NEW rides/orders only. - Never interrupt already accepted rides, orders or active deliveries. Preserve earnings, order and settlement history. - Expiry reminders, renewal payment history/receipt, immediate reactivation after successful renewal, and Admin manual extension. - Admin-configurable values later. ## 11. Future Admin settings - Razorpay credentials will be supplied later; store only in Supabase secrets/server-side configuration, never HTML. - GSTIN will be supplied later and must be editable in Admin settings. - Twelve free/support phone numbers will be supplied later and editable in Admin settings. - APITXT remains the SMS OTP provider. WhatsApp API is excluded from initial scope. ## 12. Back-test checklist 1. Customer OTP login and Admin login still work. 2. Driver and Partner registration/login; pending approval and Admin approval/rejection. 3. Partner cannot see another partner's products/orders. 4. Multi-shop cart creates correct child orders and one checkout. 5. Two partners cannot accept the same child order. 6. Priority escalation happens after five minutes; rejection routes onward. 7. Driver is notified only after Ready for Pickup; two drivers cannot accept same delivery. 8. Delivery OTP: valid, invalid, expired, repeated attempts and duplicate submission. 9. PDF invoice content, invoice numbering, QR verification, WhatsApp share, download and invoice history. 10. Refund/credit note and invoice immutability. 11. Delivery distance slabs and Admin rate changes. 12. Platform fee calculation, once-per-checkout behaviour and refunds. 13. COD ledger, cash remittance, UPI server verification and settlement reconciliation. 14. Subscription expiry, 3-day grace, suspension of new work and completion of existing work. 15. In-app notifications and background push on supported devices; deduplication and retry. 16. RLS/security tests for Customer, Partner, Driver and Admin; no service-role keys in frontend. ## 13. Build and deployment order - Treat the V7 HTML as the UI foundation; inspect and merge changes rather than replacing it with a reduced demo. - Review existing Supabase schema, triggers, RLS and current V8 SQL before applying any new migration. - Apply additive SQL in a backup/staging project first; resolve schema conflicts before production. - Deploy required Edge Functions and secrets; then integrate the final HTML/PWA. - Run the full back-test checklist before calling V8 ready.
