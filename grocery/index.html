Important Disclaimer

FRIEND RIDE is a grocery marketplace/merchant interface and is not a bank, credit card issuer, credit bureau or lender under this proposed model. FD opening, lien, secured card issuance, card limit, creditworthiness, card bill, EMI conversion and repayment are exclusively governed by the issuing bank's terms. FRIEND RIDE does not guarantee card approval or a CIBIL score outcome. All card payments are subject to bank and payment gateway authorisation. Seller invoices, GST treatment, delivery charges, cancellations and refunds will be shown separately. Promotional draws remain disabled until legal approval.
