Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FRIEND RIDE ("we", "our" or "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the FRIEND RIDE mobile application, website and related services.

By using FRIEND RIDE, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact details: mobile number used for OTP sign-in, name and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Location information: device location, including precise location when you grant permission, to support ride booking, pickup and drop-off, delivery, nearby services and live trip features. You can manage location permission in your device settings; some features may not work without it.
  • Booking and transaction details: rides, orders, service requests, addresses, booking history, payment status, refunds and support communications.
  • Profile and verification information: photographs and information submitted by drivers, partners or service providers for registration and verification.
  • Device and usage information: app/device identifiers, logs, crash diagnostics, notification tokens and interactions needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users through OTP.
  • Connect customers with drivers, delivery partners, shops and service providers.
  • Process bookings, orders, payments, invoices, refunds and customer support.
  • Enable pickup/drop-off, navigation and live location features where applicable.
  • Send essential service messages, OTPs, booking updates and notifications.
  • Verify accounts and prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve FRIEND RIDE and meet legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

We share only information needed to provide the requested service:

  • Drivers and service partners: relevant customer name, contact, pickup/delivery location and booking details.
  • Customers: relevant driver/partner identity, profile and service progress information.
  • Shops and service providers: order or service details required to fulfil a request.
  • Service providers: trusted technology, hosting, authentication, notification, analytics and payment providers that help operate the app.
  • Authorities: where required by law, legal process or to protect users and the public.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Payments

Payments may be handled by third-party payment service providers. Payment credentials are processed by the relevant provider under its terms and privacy policy. FRIEND RIDE may receive transaction references, payment status and related details needed to confirm and support a transaction.

5. Location and background access

Location may be used while the app is in use for nearby services, ride matching, pickup/drop-off and delivery. If a feature requires location updates during an active trip or task when the app is in the background, the app will request the relevant permission and use it for that feature. You can revoke permission in your device settings, although location-dependent features may become unavailable.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and comply with applicable law. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You may update profile information in the app where those controls are available, manage device permissions, and request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

To make a privacy request, contact us at noreply@friendride.in. Please do not send passwords or OTPs by email.

8. Children

FRIEND RIDE is not intended for children under 18 to create accounts or independently book services. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Third-party services and links

The app may rely on third-party services or link to third-party websites. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. Please review those policies before using their services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
